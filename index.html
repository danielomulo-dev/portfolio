# Case Study 01 · Creative

Tabasamu Sips

The problem

A new premium Kenyan rooibos kombucha had a great product but no identity, no packaging system, and no way to look premium on a shelf or a feed.

What I built

A full brand system — logo, a flavour colour-coding system, typography, packaging mockups for every variant, lifestyle marketing imagery, and a social template kit — built on an "urban artisan" brand strategy.

The result

A cohesive, premium brand the founder can extend across products, retail, and campaigns without guessing — one visual language end to end.

# Case Study 02 · Technical

Empower Africa Plugin Platform

The problem

A growing B2B trade platform on WordPress needed membership conversion, tender and investment matching, and AI assistance — without ever modifying BuddyBoss, Directorist, or MemberPress cores, and with no build step allowed on the host.

What I built

A suite of 20+ first-party plugins: a conversion-focused membership experience with plan cards and profile gating, directory-aware AI assistants, automatic dashboard integration via neutral action hooks, and a tiered investment-filter engine — all output escaped, all access fail-closed.

The result

A maintainable, upgrade-safe platform where every feature ships as a clean ZIP, passes lint, and survives third-party updates untouched.

# Case Study 03 · Creative + Data

Empower Africa Business Center, Freetown

The problem

A new event hall and private-office space in Freetown, Sierra Leone needed bookings — but early ad spend was producing leads that never converted.

What I built

Paid Meta and Google Search campaigns with tailored creative and landing pages, then a clear performance analysis that reframed the real issue as audience match and lead quality — not follow-up — and proposed a lookalike + retargeting pivot.

The result

An honest, board-ready read on what the spend actually bought and a concrete strategy to turn qualified attention into bookings.

# Case Study 04 · Creative

London Consult — Brand Identity for an Architecture Firm

The problem

London Consult is an up-and-coming architectural firm competing for mid and high-end clients in a crowded market. Their existing logo looked dated and didn't signal the premium, credible studio they wanted to be.

What I built

I benchmarked the identities of leading global architecture firms, then designed a new brand from the ground up: a clean geometric "LC" monogram that reads as premium but stays approachable, a colour system (slate blue and warm tan as primaries, with gold, black and white as supports), Metropolis as the brand typeface, and a full application set — stationery, brand collateral, apparel, and portrait/landscape layout rules.

The result

A confident, cohesive identity that lets London Consult look established and trustworthy from the first impression, with a consistent visual language that scales across everything from a business card to a full brand brochure.
